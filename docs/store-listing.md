## Core idea

Wupiko is a no-snooze alarm that gives you a reason to get up. After the alarm, you feed Wupiko real light (room lights or a window), which gets you out of bed. Then it shares where it traveled last night: a new chapter of a story every morning, and a keepsake you only get if you stay up. Good mornings grow Wupiko's island; nothing is ever taken away.

## App Review notes (iOS)

Wupiko intentionally has no snooze. Each alarm also schedules AlarmKit follow-ups at +1, +3 and +6 minutes. Stopping the system alert does not cancel them; finishing the short in-app wake-up ritual does. This is the app's core feature and is explained in the alarm editor. No sign-in is required. To test: create an alarm two minutes ahead, lock the device, stop the alert, open Wupiko and complete the ritual.

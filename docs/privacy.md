# Wupiko privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Wupiko is an alarm clock that works entirely on your device. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What stays on your device

- Your alarms: time, label, sound, repeat days and on/off state.
- Your preferences: appearance, onboarding progress and default alarm settings.
- Your wake-up history: whether each alarm was checked in on time, late or missed, used for the streak, garden and mascot outfits.

This information is stored in the app's private storage. It is not backed up to our servers, because there are none. On Android, Wupiko opts out of cloud backup. Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

## What Wupiko does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.
- No network access. The Android release build does not request the internet permission.

## Permissions

- **Alarms (iOS AlarmKit, Android exact alarms):** to ring at the times you choose.
- **Notifications and full-screen alerts (Android):** to show the ringing alarm, including over the lock screen.
- **Run at startup (Android):** to restore your alarms after the phone restarts.

You can revoke these in your device settings at any time. Alarms will not ring without them.

## Children

Wupiko does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

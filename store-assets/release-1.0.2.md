# Wupiko 1.0.2 — App Store copy (saved as a draft in App Store Connect)

## What's New in This Version

Wupiko got a lot more fun to wake up with:

• Feed Wupiko light: after your alarm, turn on the room lights or face a window for a few seconds to warm Wupiko up and check in.
• The Missing Sunrise: every morning Wupiko brings back a new story chapter. Stay up to hear what happened next and unpack the keepsake it found.
• Gentle pings when the story and the keepsake are ready, so you never miss them.
• Wupiko's island grows with every good morning and earns outfits along the way. Missed mornings only bring a little rain, nothing is ever taken away.
• Wake-ups and reminders: set reminders for lunch, water or meds that ring once, with no check-in.
• Stopping a wake-up alarm now opens Wupiko straight to the check-in.
• Fixed: the app no longer gets stuck after you allow alarm access. The app is also smaller.

## Description (updated sections)

FEED WUPIKO LIGHT
Wupiko wakes up frozen. Turn on the room lights or face a window for a few seconds and watch it warm up. A dim bedside lamp won't do, so you have to actually get up. Go back to sleep instead, and the alarm rings again after 1, 3 and 6 minutes.

A NEW STORY EVERY MORNING
While you sleep, Wupiko travels. Each morning it comes back with the next chapter of The Missing Sunrise: a postcard right away, the story a few minutes later, and a keepsake you can only unpack if you stay up. A gentle ping lets you know when each part is ready.

WATCH YOUR ISLAND GROW
Every good morning grows Wupiko's island, from the first sprouts to a pond, a little hut, a lighthouse and a rainbow, and earns outfits for Wupiko along the way. Missed mornings only bring a little rain. Nothing is ever taken away.

WAKE-UPS AND REMINDERS — wake-ups get the full check-in; reminders for lunch, water or meds just ring once.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN — adds: "The camera only measures how bright it is: no photos are taken, stored or sent."
